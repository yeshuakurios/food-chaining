Accepted Foods

Use commas between foods. Inside each entry, use food, food score, or food brand score.

How to score (optional, 1–5): 5 = a favorite, always eats happily 3 = default if you skip a score 1 = barely tolerates it

Scores help pick the next closest food step — they are never shown to your child.

Format examples: oatmeal, "banana pancakes" 4, crackers Ritz 5, "mac and cheese" "Boar's Head" 5. Separate foods with commas. Quote multi-word foods, and quote multi-word brands or use the legacy food|brand|score format.
